Annual report

The 2025 Report, Summarized

Recovery continued—and management became more complex.

California ended 2025 with nine confirmed packs and at least 55 wolves, a 10% increase from 2024. Four new packs were recognized, 19 wolves were captured for collaring, and 12 dispersal events were documented.

Population growth 55 minimum wolves, up 10%
Reproduction At least 32 pups in six packs
Monitoring 19 wolves captured for collaring
Movement 12 documented dispersal events

Livestock conflict also increased.

CDFW investigated 267 reported incidents; 198 were classified as confirmed or probable wolf-caused losses. The state compensation program distributed about $355,000 to 38 applicants.

The report also documents California's first recent lethal management removal, a mange outbreak in the Yowlumni Pack, and expanded university research.
