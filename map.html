California wolf activity map

Approximate pack territories and areas of documented wolf activity across California.

Map image: California Department of Fish and Wildlife.

How to read it

Solid gold areas are pack territories. Red hatching marks an area of wolf activity. Boundaries are approximate and may overlap.

Source and updates

Created by the California Department of Fish and Wildlife using GPS collars, trail cameras, tracks, genetics, and confirmed sightings.

The southern California panel no longer shows a pack territory because Yowlumni is under review. CDFW's July 2026 materials report no detections there in 2026 except CA091.
