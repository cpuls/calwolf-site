About CalWolf

Clear, sourced information about the return of gray wolves to California.

Hero image: CalWolf.

CalWolf provides a clear, balanced overview of the return of gray wolves to California, drawing on public agency reports and scientific research.

The goal is simple: make reliable information about California’s wolves accessible to everyone—from people encountering the subject for the first time to those who follow wolf recovery closely.

CalWolf summarizes official reports, scientific studies, and other trusted sources in an easy-to-understand format, while linking directly to original publications for readers who want to explore further.

CalWolf is not affiliated with the California Department of Fish and Wildlife or the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.
